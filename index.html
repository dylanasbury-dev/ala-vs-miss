Alabama Dominates Miss ST

The Alabama Crimson Tide roll into Starkville Mississippi ranked #8 to play the #16 ranked Mississippi Bulldogs and the bulldogs must have left their defence at home as they lose 56-23. The crimson tide scared 14 points in every quater and outside of a few turnovers early on, They were in complete control of this game from start to finish. Alabama jumped out to a 14 point lead after a 15 yard touchdown pass from Keelon Russell to Ryan Coleman-Williams and a pick six by Desmond Lee. Now to the second quarter keelon russell rushes for 17 yard touchdown but after a muffed punt by Alabama Mississippi ST has the ball inside of the red zone as Kamario Taylor finds Zion Ragins for an 11 yard touchdown. As the first half whines to a close Alabama's Tony Brown scores a 7 yard rushing touchdown and Mississippi ST kicks a last second field goal. And just like the first half Alabama pops right out scoring 14 points within the first 7 minutes with a 13 yard pass from Russell to RJ Scott and another rushing touchdown from tony brown this time from 8 yards out. Mississippi ST's Kamario Taylor hits Anthony Evans for a 45 yard touchdown late in the third quarter. But it was too little too late as Kamario Russell would leave the game earl in the fourth quarter while Alabama would score 14 more points bringing the final score to 56-23.
